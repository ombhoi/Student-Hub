Help & Resources

How can we help you?

Find quick answers to common questions about campus events, registrations, student accounts, and academic services.

search
How do I register for campus events? expand_more

To register for campus events, navigate to the "Events" tab on the navigation bar. Browse the calendar or list view to find an event of interest. Click on the event card and select the "Register" button. You will receive a confirmation notification shortly after.

Can I cancel my registration for an event? expand_more

Yes, you can manage and cancel your registered events directly from your Student Dashboard under the "My Registered Events" tab with one click.

What should I do if I forget my login credentials? expand_more

Click on the 'Forgot password?' link on the Login page to reset your password securely via your registered university student email ID.

How do I update my profile details and department information? expand_more

Navigate to your Profile page, update your personal information or department details, and click 'Save Changes' to update your campus records.

Who do I contact for administrative or technical support? expand_more

You can submit an inquiry through our Contact page or reach out directly to university IT operations support at support@studenthub.edu.

Didn't find your answer?

Our student help desk and administrative team are always ready to assist you with specific inquiries.
